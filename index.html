Weekly BD Rhythm
10–14 hours per week. Expand each day for tasks.
MON
Intelligence — 45 min
Review client target list. Update trigger event notes. Identify one new company to add to pipeline. Read one piece of industry news about a top-5 target.
TUE
Outreach — 8–10 messages
Mix of former client reactivations, referral source touches, and direct prospect outreach. Every message personalized. Never use "I hope this finds you well."
WED
Positioning — 60 min
Update one document based on a conversation from the prior week. Customize a proposal for one active prospect. Write one LinkedIn post — observation, not announcement.
THU
Relationships — 2 hours
One call with a referral source or network contact. One CPA/banker/broker outreach. Deep-dive on two target companies — know their business before you call.
FRI
Pipeline Review
Update target tracker. Identify what is stalled and why. Run the Weekly Briefing above. Schedule next week's BD time as calendar blocks before you close your laptop.
